# What is Input?

Input is an open-source, no-code form builder designed to give you full control over your forms and data. Whether you're using our hosted plans or self-hosting, Input ensures your information remains secure and accessible only to you.

![Input Dashboard](https://placehold.co/600x400?text=Input+Dashboard)
_The Input dashboard: Your central hub for form management_

## Why We Created Input

We developed Input because we believe collecting information should be straightforward and secure. Many existing form builders are either too complex or don't offer enough control over data. Input addresses these issues by providing:

1. A no-code solution for easy form creation
2. Open-source architecture for transparency and customization
3. Privacy-first approach to data management

## Key Features

### Easy Form Creation and Management

Input allows you to create and manage forms without any programming skills. Our intuitive interface makes it simple to build forms that suit your needs.

### Open-Source Flexibility

As an open-source platform, Input gives you the freedom to examine the code, contribute to its development, or even modify it to fit your specific requirements.

### Freedom from Vendor Lock-In

Unlike proprietary form builders, Input doesn't tie you to a single vendor. You're free to export your data and forms at any time, ensuring you're never stuck with a service that no longer meets your needs.
