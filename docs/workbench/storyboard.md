# Outline

The outline is a short list of your form's questions and groups. Use it to jump to a question in a long form. It replaces the Storyboard of earlier versions.

## Where to find it

-   **Wide screens (1280 px and up)** - The outline sits on the left of the editor
-   **Tablets and phones** - Click **Outline** above the questions to open it as a panel. Close it with the close button or Escape

## Using the outline

-   Each question shows its number and the start of its text. Groups show their name, with their questions below
-   Click a question to open it in the document, scrolled into view, with the cursor in its text
-   The open question is marked, and the outline keeps it in view

To add, move or delete questions, use the document itself. See [The Form Editor](./workbench.md).
