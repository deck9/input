# Managing Forms

The dashboard provides a centralized location for managing all your forms. Here you can view, organize, and take actions on your forms efficiently. To create a new form, simply click the "Create Form" button located at the top of the dashboard.

## Form Overview

When you access the dashboard, you'll see a list of all the forms you've created. Each form entry displays key information at a glance, as well as quick insights into each form's performance

## Filtering Forms

To help you find specific forms quickly, you can filter your forms by status:

1. Look for the filter option near the top of the form list.
2. Click on the status you want to filter by:
   - Draft: Shows forms that are still in progress
   - Published: Displays forms that are live and accepting submissions
   - Deleted: Lists forms that have been removed but not permanently deleted
   - No filter: Shows all forms, except for those that are deleted

## Sorting Forms

You can sort your forms to organize them in a way that's most useful to you. Sort options include:

- Name (alphabetical order)
- Created date
- Last updated date

## Form Actions

Each form has an actions menu that allows you to manage it directly from the dashboard. To access these options:

1. Locate the actions menu next to each form.
2. Click on the menu to reveal the following options:
   - Edit: Opens the form builder to make changes
   - Publish/Unpublish: Toggles the form's visibility to respondents
   - Delete: Moves the form to the deleted status (can be recovered)
   - Duplicate: Creates an exact copy of the form
